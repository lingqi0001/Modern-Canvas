# Privacy Policy for Modern Canvas

**Last Updated: October 18, 2026**

Modern Canvas (the “Extension”) helps personalize Canvas LMS and offers optional AI, account, and feedback features. This policy explains what information the Extension handles, when it leaves your browser, and the choices available to you.

## 1. The short version

Most Modern Canvas features run locally in your browser. Theme changes, layout preferences, theater view, navigation improvements, and local assignment analysis do not send your Canvas content to us.

Some optional features do transmit information:

- AI features send the content needed to generate an answer or guide to the AI service you choose or to our cloud AI service.
- Google sign-in and email sign-in use Supabase to authenticate an account and retrieve plan and credit information.
- Announcements, ratings, and survey replies are sent to our Cloudflare Worker only when you submit feedback.

We do not sell personal information, use advertising trackers, or use your Canvas data for targeted advertising.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Modern Canvas stores information locally in your browser or in Chrome extension storage. This can include theme and layout preferences, custom Canvas-domain settings, task and assignment caches, locally generated study guides, AI conversation history, learning progress, model usage records, and announcement states such as dismissed or snoozed items. The custom Canvas-domain allowlist is stored with `chrome.storage.sync` and may sync across Chrome browsers signed in to the same account. This sync setting contains hostnames chosen by you; it does not sync Canvas page content, grades, or assignment data.

AI API keys, cloud sign-in tokens, account profiles, AI chats, progress, and usage logs are stored in Chrome extension storage rather than Canvas page storage. Canvas websites cannot read Chrome extension storage. You can clear AI chats, learning progress, AI model slots, usage logs, and other extension settings from the Privacy & storage and reset controls in Settings.

## 3. Canvas content and AI features

AI features are optional and are used only after you request an AI action, such as generating an assignment guide, summarizing material, creating practice content, or drafting an Inbox reply.

Depending on the feature you use, the Extension may send relevant text that you select or that is needed to complete the request. This may include assignment titles, instructions, rubrics, course-page text, attached-file text extracted locally in your browser, your prompt, selected Inbox message text, and the draft or context needed for an AI response. If you deliberately use an image-capable AI feature with an image, that image may also be sent to the selected AI provider.

Modern Canvas uses OpenRouter as its cloud AI service provider. When you use a Modern Canvas cloud AI plan, the AI request is sent through our Cloudflare Worker gateway to obtain the requested model response, and the request may include your Modern Canvas account email, authentication token, and routing preference so that access and credits can be managed. OpenRouter and the underlying model provider process AI request content under their own policies.

AI output is generated by a model and may be inaccurate. You are responsible for reviewing AI-generated assignment guides and replies before relying on or sending them. In particular, Inbox drafts are never sent automatically by Modern Canvas.

## 4. Your own API key

You may choose to configure your own API key and model provider in Settings. Your API key is stored only in Chrome extension storage on your device. It is not uploaded to, transmitted through, or stored on Modern Canvas servers.

When you use your own API key, the Extension sends the AI request directly from your browser to the provider endpoint you selected, such as OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Moonshot, or another compatible endpoint. That provider's privacy policy and terms apply to the request. Modern Canvas does not proxy those bring-your-own-key requests through its servers.

## 5. Google sign-in, email sign-in, and plans

Google sign-in is optional. It uses Google’s OAuth flow through Supabase authentication. Modern Canvas does not receive or store your Google password. If you sign in with Google or use email verification, Supabase processes the sign-in information needed to authenticate you, including your email address, account identifier, and authentication session.

For cloud AI plans, Modern Canvas may retrieve and store your account email, account identifier, authentication token, plan status, credit allowance, credits used, and reset information. This information is used to sign you in, show plan status, and authorize cloud AI usage. Authentication sessions are stored in Chrome extension storage on your device.

## 6. Announcements, ratings, surveys, and replies

Modern Canvas may retrieve announcement and survey definitions from our Cloudflare Worker. If there is no configured announcement content, no announcement is displayed. The Extension stores a random local client identifier and local display state so it can remember dismissed, completed, or deferred announcements. The Worker receives the request for the feed and can therefore receive normal network metadata such as your IP address and request headers. Remote content is intended for product announcements and voluntary product feedback only; it is not intended to ask for passwords, payment details, grades, or sensitive student records.

Ratings, survey answers, bug reports, replies, and feature suggestions are sent to our Cloudflare Worker only when you choose to submit them. A submitted feedback record can include the announcement identifier, your answers, the feedback type, the random client identifier, and submission time. The Worker operator can access submitted feedback for support and product improvement; contact us at the address below to request deletion. Please do not include sensitive personal, academic, financial, or health information in free-text feedback fields.

Before the first AI request to a destination, Modern Canvas explains what material is shared and asks for approval. Cloud and direct-provider destinations have separate approvals. You can revoke these approvals in Settings → Privacy & storage. Revoking approval stops future requests until you approve again; it does not retract previously sent material or cancel requests already in progress.

## 7. Other external requests

Theme catalogs include preview and background image URLs hosted by third parties. When a preview is displayed or you apply a theme that uses an external image, your browser requests that image directly from its host. The host can receive your IP address, request headers, and possibly referrer information, and can change or remove the image.

If you choose a Google Fonts link or font name in Settings, your browser may request the selected font from Google Fonts. These theme-image and font requests are direct browser requests to the respective providers. Theme lists and theme JSON are retrieved from our Supabase service. Browsing or applying themes may load previews and images from third-party hosts, which receive ordinary network metadata such as your IP address and potentially referrer information. These requests are not used by Modern Canvas for advertising analytics.

## 8. Chrome permissions

Modern Canvas uses Chrome permissions for the following purposes:

- **storage**: save extension preferences, AI keys, optional account sessions, local study history, and feature state on your device.
- **identity**: complete the optional Google OAuth sign-in flow.
- **scripting**: apply Modern Canvas scripts and styles to user-authorized Canvas LMS pages, including custom school Canvas domains.
- **tabs**: identify Canvas tabs and deliver the optional Focus timer to open website tabs. This does not upload browsing history.
- **host permissions**: Modern Canvas requests access to all URLs at installation to support custom Canvas domains, external resource retrieval, and the optional all-websites Focus timer. Canvas features are injected on supported or configured Canvas domains. Only the Focus script and its styles are registered globally while a session is pinned to all websites; ending the session or turning off that mode removes the global registration. This does not revoke the extension-wide host permission.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

Data saved by the Extension remains on your device until you clear it, reset the Extension, remove the Extension, or a feature’s retention setting deletes it. AI chat history has a configurable retention period, with a default local retention period of 90 days. Some feature settings and caches are stored in the Canvas page's own `localStorage`, which belongs to that Canvas website origin and may remain after the Extension is removed; uninstalling the Extension does not guarantee that page-side data is erased. Clear site data in your browser or use the Extension's reset controls before uninstalling if you also want to remove those values.

Cloud AI account and plan records are retained by Supabase and our service providers as needed to operate the account and plan. Submitted feedback is retained in our Cloudflare Worker storage until it is deleted under our operational retention practices. You may request deletion of account-related information or submitted feedback using the contact details below.

## 10. Security

We use Chrome extension storage for sensitive local values such as API keys and authentication sessions. No system can guarantee absolute security, and you should protect your browser profile and API keys. Do not share API keys or authentication tokens with others.

## 11. Children and education records

Modern Canvas is designed for use with Canvas LMS, but it is not intended to collect sensitive education records through feedback forms. Do not submit sensitive personal information in AI prompts or feedback. Schools and users remain responsible for determining whether use of third-party AI services is permitted under their applicable policies.
