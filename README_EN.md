## 🤖 Built-in AI Features & Entry Points

Modern Canvas deeply integrates smart academic workflows with dedicated AI entry points:

### 1. 📬 Inbox AI Reply
- Draft context-aware responses and messages to your teachers directly inside Canvas Inbox.
- ⚠️ **Hard-Earned Lesson / PSA**: Please, **DO NOT** casually select the **Playful** tone!  
  *True story from testing:* While testing this feature, I accidentally selected the playful/teasing tone and accidentally sent it straight to my teacher... I literally had to rush to apologize early the next morning. Luckily, my teacher knew I was working on extension testing, lol! **Please stick to the Professional tone when emailing your teachers!**

### 2. 📝 Assignment Guide
- **Smart Difficulty Evaluation**: The extension automatically analyzes the complexity and grading scale of your assignment first, intelligently deciding whether to invoke AI assistance so you don't waste tokens on trivial tasks.
- **Rubric-Driven Breakdown**: Deeply parses assignment instructions and grading rubrics to generate clear breakdown suggestions, key scoring criteria, and an actionable step-by-step checklist to help you tackle large projects with confidence.

### 3. 🧠 Study & Retrieval Assistant
- **Teacher's Materials & Doc Ingestion**: Feed course slides, lecture notes, PDFs, or handouts distributed by your teachers into the AI to quickly distill core concepts and summarize lecture topics.
- **Task-Integrated Study Aids**: Cross-references your upcoming tasks, assignments, and exams to automatically generate customized **Cheat Sheets** or targeted **practice questions/quizzes**.

## 📦 Manual Installation (Download ZIP)

If you prefer installing it manually without going through the Web Store:

1. Click the green **Code** button at the top right of this repository and choose **Download ZIP** (or download the `.zip` package from the [Releases](https://github.com/lingqi0001/Modern-Canvas/releases) page).
2. Unzip the downloaded file to any folder on your computer (e.g., your Desktop).
3. Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/` in the address bar.
4. Enable the **Developer mode** toggle in the top-right corner.
5. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner and select the unzipped folder. That's it!
